## Audio

- Music: `public/assets/audio/music/1.mp3`, `2.mp3`, `3.mp3` were supplied by the site owner. Their licence was
  not checked by the developer: confirm you hold the rights before publishing.
- Sound effects and ambience (footsteps, pull-cord click, clinks, murmur, UI ticks) are synthesised at runtime
  with the Web Audio API. No audio samples are used.

## Everything else

Furniture, tableware, the building, the exterior and all textures (parquet, marble, artwork, menu card,
skyline windows) are generated procedurally in code. Reflections use three.js `RoomEnvironment`; the sky is
the three.js `Sky` shader. Fonts: Inter and Cormorant Garamond (SIL Open Font License, self-hosted via
`@fontsource`). Libraries: three.js (MIT), GSAP (standard no-charge licence), lil-gui (MIT, debug only).
